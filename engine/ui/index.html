How it works

Three steps, about three minutes.

  1. 01

    Paste your URL

    Staging, preview or production. Choose how many agents and how long they get.

  2. 02

    The swarm attacks

    Each agent has a personality: the rage-clicker, the phone user, the form-filler, the commuter on a dead connection. You watch every one of them live.

  3. 03

    Get bugs and fixes

    Crashes, failed requests, blank screens, frozen tabs, broken links and layouts that spill off the screen, each with a suggested fix.

What you get back

Evidence, not a vague bug ticket.

A screenshot of every failure

Captured the instant the page broke, from the agent's own browser, at the screen size it was using.

Exact steps to reproduce

Replay them in a fresh browser to prove the bug is real, and shrink them to the shortest list that still breaks it.

Fixes written by Nemotron 3 Ultra

Ultra reads the error, the page and the steps, explains the cause in plain words, and proposes the change.

Real code diffs, if you share the source

Point it at your project folder and the fix becomes a diff you can apply, verify by replaying, and undo.
